This module adds a new section to the Odoo Portal, "My Consigned Stock",
allowing owners of consigned goods (owner_id) to independently check the
availability of their own products.

Isolation of information between owners is guaranteed for every quantity
shown: on hand and free stock rely on the standard ``owner_id`` concept
of ``stock.quant``, while incoming and outgoing quantities are computed
from the pending stock moves whose originating operation
(``stock.picking``) or reservation is linked to the authenticated owner.
Forecasted stock is derived exclusively from these owner-isolated
figures. Products are only listed when they have identifiable stock or
pending movements for the authenticated owner.

Configuration
=============

For a third party to be able to check their consigned stock from the
Portal:

1. Create or identify the ``res.partner`` that will act as the owner of
   the goods.
2. Register the corresponding stock in ``stock.quant`` by setting the
   ``owner_id`` field to that partner (via the standard
   reception-with-ownership processes, or via the "Update Quantity"
   wizard specifying the owner).
3. Grant Portal access to a user linked to that partner (or to a child
   contact of the same commercial entity).

No additional configuration is required: the module does not introduce
any system parameters or specific settings.

Usage
=====

For the Portal end user:

1. Access the Customer Portal (``/my``) with an authenticated user.
2. If the user has stock associated as an owner (``owner_id``), the "My
   Consigned Stock" entry will be displayed.
3. Upon entering, the list of products will be shown with the
   availability figures corresponding exclusively to their owner:

   - On hand stock
   - Free stock
   - Incoming
   - Outgoing
   - Forecasted stock

4. It is possible to search by name or internal reference, sort the list
   and filter by warehouse when several applicable warehouses exist.

Selecting a warehouse never expands the accessible scope of information:
it only allows narrowing the query within the set already authorized for
the authenticated owner.
