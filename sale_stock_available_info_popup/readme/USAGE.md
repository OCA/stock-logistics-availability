To use this module, you need to:

1.  Go to *Sales \> Orders \> Quotations* and create a new one.
2.  Add a sale order line with a storable product.
3.  Click on the chart icon of the line and you will see in the popover
    the 'Available to promise' quantity of the corresponding product.

Note: the quantity is computed at the line level, so if the same product
is added in several lines, every line discounts the quantities of the
previous ones. The commitment date of the order and the warehouse of the
line are taken into account as well.
